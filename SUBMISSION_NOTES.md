# Submission notes

The package is formatted as a generic high-standard theory/methods submission rather than for a named journal. Before submission, adapt word count, reference style, title page, figure placement, data-availability language and cover letter to the selected journal.

A Q1-style structure and falsification standard do not guarantee acceptance or quartile placement. The present paper is a **synthetic theory/methods paper**; a strong neuroscience journal may require empirical dual-EEG validation before accepting the central ICE application.

Recommended pre-submission actions:
1. Select target journal and apply its author guidelines.
2. Preregister the prospective empirical test separately; do not merge future empirical results into the synthetic study post hoc.
3. Deposit code/results in a persistent repository and replace repository placeholders with DOI/commit hash.
4. Verify authorship, affiliation, funding, conflict-of-interest and institutional ethics language.
5. Obtain an independent mathematical/statistical review of the quotient and CCA claims.
